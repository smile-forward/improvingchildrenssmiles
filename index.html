Our Mission

For many of us, brushing our teeth is a daily habit we don’t think twice about. But for individuals experiencing homelessness, access to basic oral health supplies is often a luxury they simply don’t have.

This fundraiser was created to provide essential oral hygiene kits, including toothbrushes, toothpaste, floss, and other dental care items, to people experiencing homelessness. These simple tools do more than protect teeth; they help prevent infections, reduce pain, and restore confidence and dignity.

Why Oral Health Matters

Oral health affects overall health, confidence, and dignity. Many people experiencing homelessness lack access to dental care and basic hygiene supplies. Something as small as a toothbrush can make a real difference.
