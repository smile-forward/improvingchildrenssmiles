Restoring Smiles with Smile Forward

A student based Non-Profit organization based in Mississauga promoting oral health awareness

Our Mission

For many people, brushing their teeth is a simple daily habit. However, for individuals experiencing homelessness, access to basic oral hygiene supplies is often unavailable. Without preventative care, minor dental issues can develop into painful infections that affect overall health, confidence, and dignity. Our mission is to bridge this gap by providing essential oral health kits that empower individuals to care for themselves. Through compassion, education, and action, we aim to restore not just smiles, but confidence and well-being.

Why Oral Health Matters

Oral health is deeply connected to overall physical and mental health. Poor oral hygiene has been linked to heart disease, diabetes, respiratory infections, and chronic pain. For vulnerable populations, these risks are magnified due to limited access to dental care. By addressing oral health, we are addressing long-term health outcomes, self-esteem, and quality of life. Preventative care is one of the simplest yet most powerful tools in reducing health disparities.
