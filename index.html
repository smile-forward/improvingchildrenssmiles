Our Mission

We believe everyone deserves access to basic oral healthcare. This initiative collects donations to provide toothbrushes, toothpaste, floss, and other oral health supplies to individuals experiencing homelessness.

Why Oral Health Matters

Oral health affects overall health, confidence, and dignity. Many people experiencing homelessness lack access to dental care and basic hygiene supplies. Something as small as a toothbrush can make a real difference.

How You Can Help

Your donation goes directly toward purchasing oral health supplies and distributing them through community outreach.
