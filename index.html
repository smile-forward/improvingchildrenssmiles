Restoring Smiles with Smile Forward

A student based Non-Profit organization based in Mississauga promoting oral health awareness

Our Mission

For many people, oral hygiene is a routine part of daily life—something done almost automatically. However, for individuals experiencing homelessness, access to even the most basic oral health supplies is often limited or completely unavailable. Without toothbrushes, toothpaste, or floss, minor dental issues can escalate into painful infections and long-term health complications.

This initiative provides essential oral hygiene kits including toothbrushes, toothpaste, floss, and dental care tools. These supplies help prevent infections, reduce pain, and restore dignity while supporting overall health and confidence.

Why Oral Health Matters

Oral health is directly connected to overall physical and mental well-being. Poor oral health has been linked to heart disease, diabetes, and respiratory infections. For individuals without access to dental care, these risks increase significantly.

Preventative oral care is one of the most accessible ways to protect long-term health. Providing basic supplies empowers individuals to care for themselves and improves quality of life.
