Restoring Smiles with Smile Forward

A student based Non-Profit organization based in Mississauga promoting oral health awareness

Our Mission

For many people, brushing their teeth is a simple daily habit. However, for individuals experiencing homelessness, access to basic oral hygiene supplies is often unavailable. Without preventative care, minor dental issues can develop into painful infections that affect overall health, confidence, and dignity.

Why Oral Health Matters

Oral health is deeply connected to overall physical and mental health. Poor oral hygiene has been linked to heart disease, diabetes, respiratory infections, and chronic pain.
